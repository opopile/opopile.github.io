About

I am Xuyao Zhang (online handle: BeiChen / opopile), an independent researcher interested in understanding internal mechanisms in neural networks and turning mechanistic diagnoses into principled, zero-gradient weight-space interventions.

My recent work focuses on chunked KV-cache compression dynamics, mechanistic causal verification, and closed-form Weight-Space Programming (WSP) with explicit, auditable error constraints.
